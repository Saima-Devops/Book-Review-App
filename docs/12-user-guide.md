# Book Shelf User Guide

Developed and maintained by Saima Usman.

## Discover Books

Browse The Collection, search by title/author, and change the sort order.
Open a book to read its synopsis, catalog source, rating, and community reviews.
Browsing does not require an account.

## Create an Account

Register with a name, unique username, email, and password. Log in using username
and password. Email is used for registration and optional password recovery.
Password reset availability depends on configured email delivery.

## Add a Book

While signed in, enter a title, author, and synopsis. Catalog suggestions can
fill known details; review and edit them before submitting. Manual entry remains
available. Select Upload cover to choose an optional JPG, PNG, or WebP image
up to 5 MB. Review the preview and select Use cover, then submit Upload book.
Cancel leaves the previous selection unchanged. Images are resized automatically.
Adding a book does not upload its full text or an audio file.
Duplicate catalog works cannot be submitted repeatedly.

Only the uploader can delete a book through ordinary user controls. Deleting
a book also removes its cover and reviews and requires confirmation.

## Reviews and Ratings

Select a 1-5 star rating and submit a review on the book page. Personal reviews
can be edited or deleted. Other readers' reviews cannot be changed.
The book's average updates when reviews change.

## Favourites

Use the heart while signed in to save a book to My favourites.
Preferences belong to the current browser and account. They do not synchronize
between devices. Clearing browser storage or changing the website's domain
can make the previous list unavailable at the new origin.

## Report Content

Select Report book or Report review, choose a reason, and optionally explain
the concern. Reporting does not immediately remove content. Administrators
review reports privately and may dismiss them or remove content.
Duplicate reports for the same target/account are rejected.

## Password Recovery

Select Forgot password, enter the registered email, and follow the emailed link
when email delivery is enabled. Links expire after 30 minutes and can be used
once. Never share a reset link. An acknowledgement does not disclose whether
an account exists.

## Community Conduct

Keep contributions relevant, respectful, and appropriate. Do not post personal
information, abusive content, spam, or material without the necessary rights.
Book Shelf provides reporting and administrator moderation, not automated
content verification or a copyright clearance service.
